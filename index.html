Hi, I'm Matty

A senior level gameplay programmer with over 10 years of experience and an unstoppable passion for games.

Games have always been a huge part of my life for as long as I can remember; from the first games console I got when i was at school, to getting my first proper gaming rig PC where i got to know some of my best friends, to when I was at university relaxing after handing in the latest deadline, and now as a hobby with the free time I have after work, personal development projects, and other hobbies outside of my digital environment.

Games are wonderful adventures for everyone to enjoy, they'll take you to impossible places and tell incredible stories. You'll meet some of your greatest friends in them and in no time at all you will be telling your own stories about your experiences with them.

Games are truly wonderful and I hope I get to create them so that those who play them feel the same way as I do about games.
