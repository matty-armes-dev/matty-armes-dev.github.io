Game Credits

This page features games I've worked on, in a rough chronological order.
Starting at the oldest and moving to the newest, grouped by studio.

Codemasters / Electronic Arts

Dirt Rally 2.0

  • steam xbox playstation

Working at Codemasters was an amazing experience. My first AAA studio in my career. I joined as a junior programmer and was immediately tasked with some key areas of the project.

In-Game Achievements, the Time Trial game mode, UI screens, and various bug fixes.

Now as a junior games programmer I wasn't left alone on these tasks. I had ample support from an incredible team who paired me with other programmers to work alongside them on the tasks.

GRID(2019)

  • steam

This was my second project at Codemasters. Starting shortly after the work for Dirt Rally 2.0 wrapped up.

I was tasked with working with designers and other programmers to help bring the first time user experience to life. It was an incredibly fun task to work on as the tech involved in the unique first time user experience was special and exciting.

EA SPORTS™ WRC

Work on in-house engine code, an off the shelf engine, porting of code between engines, and being responsible for some systems as they were brought across.
